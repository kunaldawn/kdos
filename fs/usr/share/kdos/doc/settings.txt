KDOS SETTINGS

Two screens. The grid names nine categories; Enter opens one and Escape comes
back to the grid rather than leaving the program.

A page has two panes. Tab moves between them, the left one chooses a category
and the right one holds that category's fields.

Fields

  A choice field steps with the left and right arrows.
  A number field steps with the arrows and can also be typed: Enter opens the
  editor, Enter again accepts it, Escape abandons it.
  A text field is typed the same way.
  A tool row runs a program rather than storing anything; Enter opens it.

Nothing is written until you press `a`. Edits are held in the page until then,
which is why stepping back to the grid loses nothing and why the unsaved-
changes question is asked once, at the single exit.

Boxes

  The Boxes category lists the boxed applications on this machine. Enter opens
  one's profile, Escape steps back to the list, and `a` writes the profile.
  A profile that has never been written is created by `a` instead.

Appearance

  The accent, the scheme and the CRT pass are applied live: `kdos theme` is
  what the page calls, and every running surface retints without a restart.

  [[doc:display]] is a separate program — screens, modes and layout are not
  settings that can be reverted by editing a file.
