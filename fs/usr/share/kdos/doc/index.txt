The keys

  Every surface answers the same five: F1 opens the page it has, F10 opens
  its menu bar, Shift+F10 the menu of the thing under the caret, Esc steps
  back one level, and the bottom row names whatever else the surface answers
  right now. A key that is not on that row is a key the surface does not
  answer in the state it is in.
