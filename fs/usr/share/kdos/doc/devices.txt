KDOS DEVICES

Cameras, microphones, scanners and removable media, in one list.

  Enter     acts on the selected row, and what it does depends on the row:
            a camera opens a preview, a removable volume mounts or opens,
            an update row applies it. The bottom row always names which.
  u         unmounts a mounted volume.
  m         mutes every microphone, or unmutes them.
  r         rescans.

The camera preview is drawn in cells from the frames the device produces —
it is a preview, not a viewfinder. Escape takes it down and returns to the
list; Escape on the list closes the window.

Scanners

  The list is SANE's own, through `scanimage -L`, and it is the same list
  `scanimage` will scan from. A scanner that is plugged in and does not appear
  is almost always a permission: 70-kdos-scanner.rules grants the device to
  `dialout`, and without that membership SANE enumerates nothing and reports no
  error at all. `id` answers it.

  This window lists them; it does not scan. `scanimage -d <device> >out.pnm`
  is the command, and the device string is the one shown beside the model.

Removable media

  Mounting is done by kdos-mountd, which runs as root and answers a fixed set
  of verbs over a socket. It refuses to touch the medium this session booted
  from — by the whole disk, not by the partition, because a live medium
  carries a boot partition and an EFI partition and only the disk tells them
  apart.
