KDOS RES

One page per thing this machine can be short of. The list on the left names
them; the page beside it is the one in front of you.

  F10       opens the page list over the body, for a window too narrow to
            show the list beside the page.
  [ and ]   step to the previous and next page.
  Tab       moves the focus between the page list and the page.
  Esc       steps back one level: out of a detail view to its list, out of
            the page list to the page, off a chart's sample, and only then
            out of the program.
  q         leaves, except while a question or the F10 page list is up; in
            a question it refuses.

Pages

  Processes, Applications and Boxes each list something running, sorted by
  CPU unless res.conf's `sort` names another column; a click on a column
  heading sorts by it. Enter opens the detail view for the selected row,
  which is the whole frame: one subject, with the page list put away,
  because a list beside it would be offering to leave without saying so.

  CPU, Memory, GPU, Drives, Network, Batteries, Energy and Sensors are
  measurements. They sample on a timer, once a second unless res.conf's
  `interval` says otherwise. A chart keeps the last 256 samples and draws
  one per column, so a narrow chart shows fewer of them.

  On CPU and Memory a chart reads one sample at a time: rest the pointer
  on it, or press Left, and the reading top right says how long ago that
  sample was and what it read. Left steps further back, Right steps
  forward, and Escape goes back to the value now.

Questions

  Ending a process asks first, and the question owns the keyboard while it is
  up. `y` confirms, `n`, `q` and Escape refuse. Neither leaves the program: a
  dialog whose cancel key also quits is one that loses the answer.
