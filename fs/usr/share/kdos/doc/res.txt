KDOS RES

One page per thing this machine can be short of. The list on the left names
them; the page beside it is the one in front of you.

  F10       opens the page list over the body, for a window too narrow to
            show the list beside the page.
  [ and ]   step to the previous and next page.
  Tab       moves the focus between the page list and the page.
  Esc       steps back one level: out of a detail view to its list, out of
            the page list to the page, and only then out of the program.
  q         leaves, from anywhere except a question.

Pages

  Processes, Applications and Boxes each list something running, newest
  measurement first. Enter opens the detail view for the selected row, which
  is the whole frame: one subject, with the page list put away, because a
  list beside it would be offering to leave without saying so.

  CPU, Memory, GPU, Drives, Network, Batteries and Energy are measurements.
  They sample on a timer and the charts are the last two minutes.

Questions

  Ending a process asks first, and the question owns the keyboard while it is
  up. `y` confirms, `n` and Escape refuse. Neither leaves the program: a
  dialog whose cancel key also quits is one that loses the answer.
