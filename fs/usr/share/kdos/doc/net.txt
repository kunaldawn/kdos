KDOS NET

Wireless and wired in one list, through NetworkManager. The device rows come
first and the access points under them, strongest first.

  Enter     joins the selected network. On a device row it rescans instead.
            A network you are already on answers nothing — the row is where
            you see that you are on it.
  f         forgets a saved network, so the next join asks again.
  c         copies the network's name.
  a         turns wireless off, or back on.

A network that needs a passphrase asks for one on the bottom row. Escape there
abandons the join and leaves the list as it was; Escape on the list closes the
window.

What this window does not do

  Static addresses, VPNs and per-connection routing are NetworkManager's and
  are edited with `nmcli`. This window is the two things a person does at a
  desk: join something, and stop being on something.
