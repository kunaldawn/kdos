KDOS DISPLAY

One row per screen the compositor reports, in the order they are arranged
left to right.

  Space     turns the selected screen off, or back on. The last screen that
            is still on cannot be turned off — there would be nothing left to
            answer with.
  m         steps the selected screen's mode. From `auto` the first press
            lands on the mode the monitor itself advertises as preferred.
  s         steps the scale.
  t         rotates.
  [ and ]   move the selected screen earlier or later in the arrangement.
  Enter     applies the whole plan.

Nothing above touches the screen until Enter.

The fifteen seconds

  An applied plan is provisional. A countdown runs, and unless you press K
  the previous arrangement comes back. That is not a confirmation dialog
  being cautious: a mode a monitor cannot show leaves a black screen, and a
  black screen cannot be used to answer a question.

  K         keep this arrangement, and write it.
  R         revert now, without waiting.
  Escape    the same as R — reverting is what the countdown does on its own,
            so it cannot lose work.

The kept arrangement is written and applied again at the next login.
