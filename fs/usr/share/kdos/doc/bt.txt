KDOS BT

Bluetooth through bluez. One row per device the adapter knows about.

  Enter     connects the selected device, or disconnects it.
  p         pairs a device that is not paired yet.
  t         trusts it, or stops trusting it. A trusted device reconnects on
            its own; an untrusted one asks every time.
  x         removes a paired device entirely.
  s         starts a scan, or stops one.
  o         powers the adapter on or off.
  d         makes this machine discoverable, or hides it again.

Pairing

  A device that wants confirmation puts its question on the bottom row and
  bluez waits for the answer. Press `y` to confirm and `n` to refuse; Escape
  refuses too, because the question has to be answered one way or the other —
  leaving it unanswered would leave bluez blocked.
