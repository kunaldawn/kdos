title: xdg-desktop-portal snapshots its backends at startup
cite: kdos-desktop-start
port: xdg-desktop-portal-wlr

kdos-desktop-start waits for the compositor socket, pushes WAYLAND_DISPLAY
into the D-Bus activation environment, has the bus start
xdg-desktop-portal-wlr, WAITS for it to own its bus name, and only then has
the bus (re)start the main portal — or ScreenCast stays empty all session.
