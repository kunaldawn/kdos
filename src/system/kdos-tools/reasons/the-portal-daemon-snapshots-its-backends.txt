title: xdg-desktop-portal snapshots its backends at startup
cite: kdos-desktop-start
port: xdg-desktop-portal-wlr

kdos-desktop-start waits for the compositor socket, pushes WAYLAND_DISPLAY
into the D-Bus activation environment, starts xdg-desktop-portal-wlr, WAITS
for it to own its bus name, and only then (re)starts the main portal — or
ScreenCast stays empty all session.

Backend-independent, so the fix survived the move off COSMIC verbatim.
