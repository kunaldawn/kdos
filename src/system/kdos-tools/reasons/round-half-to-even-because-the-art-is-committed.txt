title: reproduce python's rounding exactly — the vendored artwork is COMMITTED
cite: libkcolor
port: kdos-theme

kcol_to_hls / kcol_from_hls / kcol_remap reproduce colorsys including the odd
`2.0 - maxc - minc`, the modulo on a negative hue, and round()'s
half-to-even. A C generator that rounds differently diffs against files
already in git. Verified over 8476 colours x 4 schemes against CPython.

kcol_mix (integer, percent) and kcol_mixf (float) are NOT interchangeable —
they disagree by a unit on some inputs, and each generated file was written
against exactly one of them.
