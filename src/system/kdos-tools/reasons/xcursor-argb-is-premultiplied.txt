title: Xcursor ARGB is premultiplied — divide alpha out before any ramp
cite: kdos-cursors
port: kdos-cursors

Recolour without dividing and multiplying back and every anti-aliased edge
turns into a dark halo.

The other cursor trap is aliasing direction: alias groups are re-rooted on
their CSS names (`default` is the real file, `left_ptr` the symlink). Bibata
ships the opposite direction, and installing it over the old theme produced
default -> left_ptr -> default, so kpkg aborted with
"realpath: Symbolic link loop".
