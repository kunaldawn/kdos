title: the median of several runs, and a win smaller than the measured noise is not a win
cite: kdos march
port: kdos-tools

The mean is owned by its worst outlier and one run measures the scheduler. The
noise floor is the spread of the samples themselves; both real measurements so
far landed inside it and were reverted, which is the correct answer on a busy
machine and the tool says so.

A port with no `bench =` line is UNMEASURABLE and never a winner — assuming a
win for it is the blind optimisation this replaces. And `bench_setup =` runs
once and is not timed: the first bench line written measured
`dd if=/dev/urandom` more than it measured lz4.
