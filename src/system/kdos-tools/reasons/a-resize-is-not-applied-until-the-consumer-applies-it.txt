title: a resize is not applied until the consumer applies it
cite: libktui
port: kdos-installer

The backend sets ktui_resized; ktui_w/ktui_h follow only when the loop calls
ktui_draw_resize() and ktui_draw_invalidate(). A loop that never needed it,
because its surface had a fixed size, starts needing it the moment that
surface can be resized: until then it draws against the old size, a guard
such as `y + rows > h` fails on content that would fit, and the frame paints
an empty box. Any loop that owns a surface owns this.
