title: only a real glyph ends fbcon's deferred takeover
cite: kdos-getty
port: kdos-tools
port: terminus-font

Escape sequences are eaten by the VT state machine and even SPACES are skipped
by the render path. Verified: ' ' -> nothing, 'K' -> takeover. kdos-getty
prints K and clears it.

The takeover then re-initialises every VT with the kernel's built-in font,
which is why a setfont in rcS is silently wiped and the font load belongs in
the getty wrapper. The takeover is scheduled work, so poll dmesg for
"fbcon: Taking over console" and retry setfont until showconsolefont -i
confirms 16x32.
