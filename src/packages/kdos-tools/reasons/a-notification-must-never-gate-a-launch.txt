gdbus's default reply timeout is 25 SECONDS. notify() uses --timeout 2 and is
backgrounded, so the toast can never sit in front of the app it is announcing.

Same family: a blind `flock -w 120` on the login warmup lock made a click wait
for the entire container init, up to two minutes of dead-looking desktop. The
launch path waits out an in-flight warmup only when the box is already running
and container_setup_done has not yet appeared in podman logs.
