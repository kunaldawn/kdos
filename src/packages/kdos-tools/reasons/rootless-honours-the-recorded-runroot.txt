title: podman RECORDS the runroot, and rootless honours it
cite: 01_appbox.sh
port: kdos-appbox
port: podman

The bake runs rootful podman with --runroot /tmp/appbox-runroot. Rootless
podman then reads that path out of the user's libpod database, dies on
"mkdir /run/libpod: permission denied", and EVERY podman call fails —
silently, because the launcher swallows stderr.

Tell-tale: `podman images` as kdos fails while the same command as root works.
01_appbox.sh removes $STORAGE/libpod and db.sql after the load; the first
rootless call recreates them.
