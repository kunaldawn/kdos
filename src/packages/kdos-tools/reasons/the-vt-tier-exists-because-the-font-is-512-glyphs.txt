ter-kdos32n has the box sets and the three shades and no eighth blocks, no
half blocks, no braille and no left/right arrows (which is why ktui_glyph
carries the triangles instead). A glyph the font does not carry renders as a
BLANK on tty1, so an eighth-block bar there is not ugly, it is missing.

Three levels is the honest resolution of that font. Grep uni/xos4-2.uni in the
terminus-font tarball before using any glyph; the port adds exactly six
codepoints to it, the double box-drawing set the block logo needs.
