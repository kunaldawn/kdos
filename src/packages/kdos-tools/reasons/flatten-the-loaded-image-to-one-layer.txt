title: flatten the image to one layer, or rebuilt dirs come up EMPTY
cite: store.c
port: kdos-appbox
port: podman

An overlay image records its whiteout and opaque markers as trusted.overlay.*,
and a ROOTLESS mount cannot see that namespace. Ship an app's layers as they
sit and every multi-layer-rebuilt directory — /etc/alternatives is the one that
bites — is empty inside the box.

`podman create` + `podman export` + tar is the flatten, and `kdos-appbox store
export` runs it before `kdos-pack build` for exactly this: the exported tarball
is one layer, so no marker and no xattr is in play and the pack mounts
unprivileged.

Tell-tale: the app starts and every alternative-managed command is "not found",
while `podman run` on the same image works.
