cite: kdos-energyd

intel-rapl:0 (a package) sits beside intel-rapl:0:0 (that package's core
domain). Measured on the fixture: 15 W becomes 26.25 W. A domain is a
subdomain exactly when it appears INSIDE another's directory. psys goes the
other way — it contains the packages, so where it exists it replaces them.

Two more that change the answer: the counter wraps about every 36 minutes at
30 W, and the idle floor must be subtracted before anything is attributed or a
machine at a login prompt reports as 90% one process.
