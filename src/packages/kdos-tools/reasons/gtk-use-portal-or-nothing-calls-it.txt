title: a GTK app routes through the portal only when it believes it is sandboxed
cite: kdos-appbox BOXENV
port: kdos-appbox

It decides that from /.flatpak-info or from GTK_USE_PORTAL=1 — and a distrobox
is neither. So FileChooser, Settings and OpenURI all existed, all answered,
and NOTHING EVER CALLED THEM: every boxed app went on drawing its own
GtkFileChooser, the exact rounded antialiased dialog kdos-pick was written to
replace.

Firefox's widget.use-xdg-desktop-portal.file-picker default of `auto` reads
the same variable. Stated cost: GTK's Print dialog goes through the portal as
well and KDOS has no Print backend — but printing from a boxed app did not
work before either.
