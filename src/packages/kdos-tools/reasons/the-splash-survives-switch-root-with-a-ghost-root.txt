title: the splash spans switch_root, and every path it resolves after is a ghost
cite: kdos-splash
port: kdos-splash
see: switch-root-ms-move

It is never chroot'ed, so afterwards its / is the deleted initramfs root whose
/dev was moved away. Open fds keep working — that is the whole trick, one
process spanning both halves of boot with the FIFO on devtmpfs — but every
path it resolves BY NAME after that points into the ghost. Hence the `quit`
client detects the daemon by opening the FIFO for writing and watching for
ENXIO, and does the cleanup itself from the real root.

Two more, one debug cycle each: deferred take-over means nothing is being
scanned out until one byte reaches /dev/tty0, and mmap writes need an explicit
FBIOPAN_DISPLAY or they reach the host whenever fbdev's deferred-IO worker
gets round to it.
