title: on a 512-glyph VT, A_BOLD changes the FONT PAGE, not the weight
cite: kinstall
port: kdos-installer

The VT steals the foreground intensity bit for the 9th glyph bit, so colours
8-15 are unreachable as foreground and bold selects the second half of the
font. The whole installer UI is therefore eight colours — which also means a
tty and a truecolor foot window render the same picture.

On a VT it saves the palette with GIO_CMAP, installs its own with PIO_CMAP and
restores exactly what kdos-getty's setvtrgb loaded; elsewhere the same slots go
out as 24-bit or 256-colour SGR.
