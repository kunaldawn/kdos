title: unknown is never folded into current — that is a confident wrong answer
cite: kdos-portup, kdos cve
port: kdos-tools

kdos-portup has three outcomes on purpose. A listing that could not be
reached, a directory listing whose tail the adapter's own cap cut off, and a
listing whose rendered URLs all 404 are all `unknown` with a reason, not
silently averaged into "up to date".

`kdos cve` keeps the same rule from the other side: a package Alpine does not
carry is UNKNOWN, never clean. 262 of 389 ports are in that state and the
summary says so; a checker that counted them as fine would be reporting a
number it had not earned.
