title: a tmpfs already mounted ignores a mode change on remount
cite: rcS
port: toybox
see: tmp-mode-1777

`mount -o remount,mode=1777 /tmp` silently does nothing. Only chmod works,
which is why rcS runs an explicit `chmod 1777 /tmp` after `mount -a` even
though fstab already says mode=1777.

This is the second half of the /tmp bug and the half that is easy to leave out
after fixing the first.
