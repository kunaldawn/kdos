title: symbols are prefixed, or two of our own programs cannot be linked together
cite: libktui extraction
port: kdos-appbox

Rect, Event, Ui, xstrdup and read_file were all unprefixed, and kdos-appbox
had already declared its own xstrdup and a read_file with different length
semantics.

Two more rules the extraction exists to keep: the frame state is private
(`ui.consumed = 1` appeared in two dozen places in the installer alone), and
chrome ids are the library's business — ui.c used to hardcode the installer's
sidebar id, and claiming real focus ids there pushed every control on every
page ten places down the Tab ring and parked the caret on a decoration.
