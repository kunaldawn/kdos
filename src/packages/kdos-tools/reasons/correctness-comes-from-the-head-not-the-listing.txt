title: correctness comes from the final HEAD, not from the discovery step
cite: kdos-portup
port: kdos-tools

A forge feed or a directory listing can name a version whose tarball lives
somewhere the recipe's template does not expect. A plain 404 drops that
candidate and the next-highest is tried; only a 200 proves NEWER.

The candidate is rendered by copying the recipe to a temp dir and expanding it
through `kpkg meta` — the same parser the build uses, so a helper chain falls
out for free and no probe ever touches the real tree.
