## About
[hyperfine]{.idx} benchmarks commands: it runs each one many times, after optional warm-up runs,
and reports the mean, the spread and the minimum and maximum, warns about statistical outliers,
and compares several commands against each other. It is the general form of the discipline
[`kdos march`](../../../../docs/kdos/04-programs/kdos-command.md#kdos-march)
applies to one question, whether a processor tuning flag is worth keeping: several runs, the
machine's own noise measured, and no difference reported that does not clear it.

## Notes
