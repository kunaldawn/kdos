## About

Realtek's share of [linux-firmware](#port-linux-firmware): the `rtw88`, `rtw89` and `rtlwifi` Wi-Fi
firmware, the `rtl_bt` Bluetooth firmware and the `rtl_nic` firmware `r8169` loads for most Realtek
Ethernet. 165 files and links, 9,580,394 bytes. The installation medium carries it.

## Flags

- `--zstd` — Compresses every `File:` entry of `WHENCE` as `copy-firmware.sh` copies it; see
  [linux-firmware](#port-linux-firmware), whose note describes the build every port of the set
  shares.
