## About

libXfixes is the client library for the XFIXES extension: regions, cursor images and names,
selection-owner notification and pointer barriers. Clipboard managers and toolkits use it to
follow selection changes, and [libXcursor](#port-libXcursor) and [libXi](#port-libXi) build on
it. See [No Xorg server, and one carve-out](../../../../docs/kdos/01-philosophy/principles.md#no-xorg-server-and-one-carve-out).
